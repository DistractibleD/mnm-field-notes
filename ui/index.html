Welcome

MnM Field Notes is a manual-entry companion for logging what you do in Monsters and Memories, so a session's kills, catches, and gathers can help update the wiki later. Everything here is typed by hand — the app never reads or touches the game itself, the same way keeping a notebook open next to it wouldn't.

How it works

  • Combat, Gathering, Fishing, and Cooking all show useful info for whatever zone you pick — named/regular monsters, expected nodes, catch-rate estimates — even before you start a session.
  • Start a session from the bar up top when you're ready to log something. Gathering and Fishing walk you through their own quick setup (zone, skill) and start one for you.
  • Logging is built for speed, not forms — mostly one-tap grids and small counters, not long fields to fill in every time.
  • "End session & export" writes a plain-text summary to the Sessions\ folder, and offers to submit it straight to the wiki for review.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your own computer unless you choose to submit it. A submission goes to the wiki's review queue as a pull request — nothing is added automatically, a human decides. The name you picked on first launch is just a plain text label attached to what you log, not an account of any kind.
